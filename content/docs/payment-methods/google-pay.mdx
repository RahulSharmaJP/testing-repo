Google Pay is available in India and Southeast Asia. The flows differ enough
between the two that most of this page is variant content.

## How it works

<Variant region="in">
In India, Google Pay runs over UPI. The customer picks Google Pay at checkout,
Juspay creates a UPI intent, and the customer approves the collect request in
their Google Pay app. Juspay receives the callback and moves the order to
`CHARGED`.
</Variant>

<Variant region="sea">
In Southeast Asia, Google Pay is a card wallet. The customer authorises in the
Google Pay sheet, which returns an encrypted payment token. That token is passed
to Juspay, decrypted by the acquirer, and processed as a card transaction. 3DS may
still be triggered, in which case the order passes through `PENDING_VBV`.
</Variant>

## Prerequisites

<Variant region="in">
- A UPI-enabled merchant account, configured in the Juspay dashboard
- Your VPA handle allowlisted by the sponsor bank
</Variant>

<Variant region="sea">
- A Google Pay merchant ID from the Google Pay & Wallet Console
- Tokenisation settings configured for your acquirer
- Your domain verified, if you are on web
</Variant>

## Integration

<Variant integrationPath="hypercheckout">
HyperCheckout renders Google Pay for you. Enable it in the dashboard, and it
appears in the payment method list once the customer's device supports it. No
client-side work is required beyond the standard HyperCheckout initialisation.
</Variant>

<Variant integrationPath="ec-sdk">
The Express Checkout SDK exposes Google Pay as a payment method you render
yourself. You control placement and styling; the SDK handles the wallet
handshake and returns the payment payload.
</Variant>

<Variant integrationPath="ec-api">
On the Express Checkout API path you own the entire wallet handshake. Collect
the payment token client-side, then submit it on the payment request. Juspay
does not render anything for you here.
</Variant>

### Platform notes

<Variant platform="web">
On web, availability depends on the browser and on whether the customer has a
saved instrument. Always call the availability check before rendering the button,
or you will show a payment method that fails on tap.
</Variant>

<Variant platform="android">
On Android, Google Pay is available through the Play Services API. The SDK
handles the binding; you need Play Services 19.0.0 or later on the device.
</Variant>

<Variant platform="ios">
On iOS, Google Pay is only available in the browser, not as a native sheet. If
you are building a native iOS app, use Apple Pay instead. This section exists
mainly to show that a variant can tell the reader a path does not apply to them.
</Variant>
