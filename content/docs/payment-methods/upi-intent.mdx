REGION-ONLY content. This page is only reachable, and only appears in the
navigation, when the region is set to India.

UPI Intent hands the customer off to a UPI app on their device with the payment
details prefilled. Unlike UPI Collect, there is no VPA entry step, so it is the
better default on mobile.

## Order states

An intent payment moves through `PENDING` while the customer is in the UPI app,
then to `CHARGED` or `AUTHORIZATION_FAILED`. Do not treat a return to your app as
success — wait for the webhook.
